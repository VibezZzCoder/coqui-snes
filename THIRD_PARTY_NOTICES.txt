   ALTERED VERSION NOTICE (zlib licence, condition 2; the SNESMod licence
   above permits modification). The SNESMod driver in this ROM is not
   byte-identical to the one PVSnesLib 4.5.0 ships: this project applies
   three patches to it after linking (60 bytes differ in all; nothing else
   in the driver changes). (1) ResetSound: the original
   writes KOFF=$FF and KOFF=$00 60 SPC cycles apart while the S-DSP polls
   KOFF every 64 cycles, so roughly one pause in sixteen never silenced the
   sounding voices; the patch reorders the same writes and clears KOFF
   through the driver's own resume-handler tail so the pulse lasts 78
   cycles, and releases voices 0-5 only, so an effect already sounding is no
   longer cut when the music stops or changes. (2) Module_UpdateChannels stops at
   channel 5, so voices 6 and 7 belong to sound effects alone (this game's
   music uses six voices). (3) Module_UpdateChannel collects the key-on bits
   of a row instead of writing KON per voice, a wrapper in the unused
   CMD_MVOL/CMD_FADE handler bytes writes KON once per tick after every
   voice's registers, and SFX_Play writes an effect's KON after its
   registers rather than before; CommandTable entries 5 and 6, which this
   ROM never sends, become acknowledged no-ops. Patches 2 and 3 exist for
   the SNES Classic's built-in emulator, on which the stock driver lost
   every effect after the first and dropped chord voices. The patches and
   their verifier are tools/patch_snesmod_koff.py in the project's
   repository. These alterations are the Coqui al Anochecer project's, not
   PVSnesLib's or SNESMod's.
